Selected work 2019 - 2024

Book of Jam: selected work

A Better Place

2023 · BBC Three · BBC Scotland · BBC iPlayer

In the sleepy Scottish fishing village of Kincully, an eccentric family makes a brave, yet bungling, attempt to save their failing funeral home from rival Eileen Toombs. With Jocelyn Jee Esien, Jonathan Watson and Darcy Montagu.

Shortlisted, Debut Director (Scripted) - New Voice Awards 2024

Also on BBC iPlayer ↗

Created, written and directed by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. Producer Laura Ferguson. Executive producers Jon Rolph, Matthew Cowan. DoP Miles Trotter. Edited by Jacob Topen. Music by Jacob Topen; “Hero in the Hearse” by Ibrahim Clayton. Commissioned by Gavin Smith (BBC Scotland) and Navi Lamba (BBC). A Production Attic production.

I Shoulda' Died!

2023 · BBC Studios

In the heart of a well-established Scottish country park, two best friends become separated by a twist of fate's destiny.

Written, directed and produced by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. DoP Miles Trotter. Sound Alison Mackenzie. PM / 1st AD Grace Austin. Edited by Jacob Topen. With Jacob Topen (Eddie, The Narrator) and Ibrahim Clayton (Vince). A BBC Studios / Dark & Stormy Films / Sufi Machine Pictures production.

Pilgrims

2023 - 2024 · Web series

Dr Kyle and Jake, last seen in The Troon Revelations, find a camera and start a church. It goes international (via Zoom), then less well.

Written and directed by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. With Ibrahim Clayton as Dr Kyle Wright and Jacob Topen as Jake.

Bon Bon Walks Off Set!

2021 · BBC Studios

Darcy Montagu is Bon Bon, a pop star with a grievance and a set of wings. Includes the official video for “Play Piper Play”.

Written, directed and produced by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. BBC Studios producers Mark Bignell & Rachel-Anne Clark. DoP Miles Trotter. With Jacob Topen, Ibrahim Clayton, Darcy Montagu and Robin MacKenzie (The Piper).

The Comeback: Now or Never

2020 · BBC Studios

A boxing comedy. John Gray is Alfie Teirnan; Julian D'mir is Kane “The Obliterator” Johnson.

Honourable Mention - London-Worldwide Comedy Short Film Festival 2021

Written, directed and produced by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. BBC Studios producer Mark Bignell. DoP Miles Trotter. Score by Jacob Topen; “Kane's Theme” by Ibrahim Clayton. With Darcy Montagu, Ross Eaglesham, Barry Mclauchlan and Ramsay Macdonald.

The Witches of the East Neuk

2020 · BBC Studios

A special investigation with Rupert D'Ssane (BA Hons).

Written, directed and produced by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. BBC Studios producer Mark Bignell. Score by Jacob Topen. With Ibrahim Clayton (Rupert D'Ssane), Jacob Topen (Ramsey, fish shop man-boy), Pamela Clayton, Darcy Montagu and Torrin Topen.

It's a Wonderful Life 2: Potter's Revenge

2020 · Sketch

Marking 75 years of the highly underrated and controversial Christmas sequel.

A Book of Jam sketch by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton.

The Troon Revelations

2019 · Short film

Dr. Kyle Wright has questions. Troon has answers. Possibly. The short film that inspired the web series Pilgrims.

Written and directed by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton. Produced with Pamela Clayton. DoP Miles Trotter. With Ibrahim Clayton, Ian Lorimer Milne, Ella Rose Taylor, Jacob Topen, Renee Williams and Pamela Clayton. A Dark & Stormy Films / Sufi Machine Pictures production.

Dear Jean

2020 · Sketch series

Granny Jean White, on air and occasionally on camera. Episodes include the dashcam footage of her selling a (big) bag of drugs. Her statement: “This woman is clearly not me. I would NEVER wear sunglasses whilst in a stationary vehicle.”

A Book of Jam sketch by Jacob Topen & Ibrahim Clayton.

About

Came here looking for a book about jam? We kindly refer you to our chief competitor… The National Trust's Book of Jam.

Now we've got the big things out of the way…

Greetings! We're the other Book of Jam.

We're comedy directors and writers, but above all, humans. We've made lots of comedy over the years, and some under them.

We've worked with big-time-tv-folk like the BBC and even won awards for our work along the way.

Do have a look around, watch our work or get in touch with us to chat about your project! You're welcome to visit back whenever you like.

With love,Jacob & Ibrahim
